Privacy, plainly.

Effective October 7, 2026

Brineloft saves your game on your device. It has no account, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

This policy describes Brineloft for iPhone and iPad and its support website, operated by Jerry Crews. The game is currently in development. Questions can be sent to jerry.crews1@gmail.com.

Game data stays on your device

Your contract records, discoveries, settings, and a record of verified expansion access are stored locally. The game does not send that data to a developer-operated server. There is no Brineloft cloud account or cross-device progress service.

Your operating system may include local game data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by you and Apple, not by Brineloft.

Purchases are handled by Apple

The optional Frozen Depths expansion uses Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and provides the game with transaction and entitlement information so it can unlock or restore the content you own. The game processes that information on your device. Brineloft does not receive your card number or Apple Account password.

Purchasing and restoring require an internet connection. Verified access is stored locally for offline use, and the game handles refunds and other changes when Apple supplies updated entitlement information. Apple may provide the developer with App Store sales and financial reports. Apple's own handling of purchase information is described in App Store & Privacy.

No advertising or behavioral tracking

Brineloft includes no advertising SDK, tracking identifier use, third-party analytics, in-app chat, social profile, or user-upload service. The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Apple may separately process and share app diagnostics or aggregate usage information according to your device privacy settings and Apple's policies. Brineloft does not add its own analytics collection. See Apple's App Analytics & Privacy information.

If you contact support

Emailing support sends your email address and whatever you choose to include to the developer and the email providers involved. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, or payment card details.

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain relevant support records. You can request deletion by emailing the same address; information that must be retained for legal obligations may be excluded.

This website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, external fonts, or embedded social widgets. It is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to Apple and email open services outside this website. Their providers apply their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Use the in-game delete-progress option to erase the current local progress.
  • Remove the app to remove its active local storage.
  • Manage device backups separately through your Apple or computer backup settings.
  • Manage purchases and refunds through Apple. Deleting progress does not revoke a purchase.
  • Email us with questions about, or requests to delete, information you voluntarily sent to support.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children and has no chat, account system, or advertising. Apple's device and purchase controls can be used to manage in-app purchases. If a child has sent personal information in a support email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. The App Store privacy disclosure will also be updated when required.
