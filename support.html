Common questions

What can I play for free?

The complete base bay contains 30 contracts across eight sites. You can finish the adventure, replay completed contracts, collect optional finds, and improve your records without making a purchase. The first Frozen Depths contract is also a permanent free sample.

How do I move and recover cargo?

Use the movement control to steer your drone. Move close to cargo and use the cable control to attach or release it. Buoyancy balloons help with heavy loads. Anchored cargo must be cut free while you hold steady nearby. Complete any required surveys and deliver cargo in the stated order and condition. After the required work is done, choose Finish dive or keep searching for relics. Recovering every object finishes automatically.

Is there oxygen or a time limit?

You pilot a robot, so it has a battery rather than an oxygen tank. Thrust, heavy loads, sonar and cutting use charge. Hold still inside the mint recovery zone to recharge; a full refill takes about seven seconds. Dock refills are free and unlimited. The target time is an optional star goal, separate from the battery. Pausing stops battery use.

How do surveys, cutting and fragile cargo work?

At a blue survey ring, pulse sonar and release steering to hold position until the survey is complete. Stay close and steady while using Cut free on an anchored load. Some contracts require deliveries in a specific order. Fragile loads must arrive above the condition threshold stated in the briefing; slow down before turns and leave room for the swinging cable.

How do I find and keep relics?

Each of the first six bay contracts has an optional relic. Explore different parts of the site and pulse sonar to reveal nearby mint RELIC markers. Tow the relic to the recovery zone, then finish the contract to save its individual story in your collection. You can keep looking after the required cargo is recovered and choose Finish dive when ready.

My battery ran out, cargo is damaged, or I am stuck. What now?

Use Recovery or restart the contract. Recovery repairs remaining cargo, recharges the drone, and returns the drone and undelivered loads to their starting positions. Delivered cargo, completed survey work and elapsed time stay. Recovery and retries are free and unlimited, with no paid energy, lives or waiting period before another attempt. Assisted completions receive one star; replay without assistance to pursue a better record.

How are stars awarded?

Completing a contract earns one star. Bring required cargo home with at least 80% integrity for another. The route-mastery star requires every optional relic and a finish within the contract's target time; contracts without relics use the time goal alone. Relics are still optional for finishing the story and unlocking the next contract. Assisted results are capped at one star. Replaying does not reduce an existing best record, including three-star results earned before an update.

Do I need the internet or an account?

Gameplay works offline and there is no Brineloft account. Apple's purchase and Restore Purchases flows need an internet connection. A previously verified expansion remains available offline; refunds or other entitlement changes are reconciled when Apple provides an update.

What does Frozen Depths include?

It contains 20 contracts across five frozen sites, including the free sample. It is designed as a permanent, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. The full pack is not yet available for sale. Its live regional price will appear in the game when Apple makes the product available.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the expansion screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and connect to the internet. Restoration unlocks eligible purchased content; it does not restore local mission progress. For a charge or refund question, visit Apple's purchase support.

Does progress sync between devices?

No. Game progress is saved on the device. Your operating system may include that data in a device backup, depending on your settings. A purchase can be restored on another device using the same Apple Account, but that does not copy your local progress.

How do I delete my progress?

Use the delete-progress option in the game's settings and confirm the reset. This removes the local game progress; it does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase. Removing the app also removes its active local data. Backups are managed separately in your device or computer backup settings.

Can I adjust sound or the controls?

Settings include sound, haptics, reduced motion, and control assistance. Important information is visible on screen so audio is not required. The game plays in landscape on iPhone and iPad.
